# MUSA 650: Final Project Proposal

In this assignment, you will outline your proposal for the final project for this semester. You will describe the problem you're seeking to solve, the proposed users and use case, data source, methodological precedent, modeling approach, and evaluation metrics.

## Submission Guidelines

**This assignment requires you to work in groups of 2-3 students. No individual assignments will be accepted. One member of the group should submit on behalf of everyone, making sure to include all group members' names at the top of the proposal.**

Submit your work as a PDF via a pull request to the main branch of this repository with the following structure:

```
assignments/
  submissions/
    FINAL_PROJECT/
      FINAL_PROJECT_PROPOSAL.pdf
```

## Proposal Requirements

Your proposal (approximately 1,000 words) must include:

- **Problem Definition & Use Case:** A clear description of the problem you're solving, including the target user and use case.
- **Methodological Precedent:** References to at least **three rigorous sources** (e.g., academic studies, professional white papers, etc.) on which you will base your methodology and approach. Summarize each source, including their methods, data sources, and any limitations or obstacles they identify.
- **Data Plan:** An explanation of what dataset(s) you plan to use, and your backup plan if that dataset is unavailable or unsuitable.
- **Modeling Approach:** A description of the model architecture you plan to use, including preprocessing steps and feature engineering. Also describe any alternative approaches you considered and why you chose your preferred method. If you have a backup model, present that as well.
- **Evaluation Strategy:** An explanation of how you will evaluate your model, including which evaluation metrics you will use and why they are appropriate for your use case and domain.
- **Group Members:** Make sure to include the names of all students in the group at the top of your proposal.
